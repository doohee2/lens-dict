examine

/ɪɡˈzæm.ɪn/

1.
verb (transitive)

To inspect or look at (something) closely and carefully in order to learn more about it, find problems, etc.

"The detective carefully examined the evidence found at the scene."

2.
verb (formal)

To test the knowledge or ability of (someone) by asking questions or making them perform tasks.
